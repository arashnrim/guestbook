# guestbook

Welcome to the repository for my guestbook! This guestbook will be a place for internet visitors to say hi and make their presence known as they come across my website(s) on the internet.

## Motivations

I've been interested in the concept of [a personal internet](https://garden.arash.codes/technology/personal-internet) for a while now, and thought of exploring different websites to piece together a definition that works for me. I also wanted to gain inspiration from these websites about how they've made their corners on the internet feel so personable and open to interaction, and stumbled across a recurring string of guestbooks.

There are already many existing guestbook services out there — a shout out to [Atabook](https://atabook.org), which seems to be a popular choice by many on NeoCities — but I wanted to keep the spirit of building in public, rediscovering the joy in creating again. What better way is there than to build something yourself?

## Contributing

If you've noticed a gap that you can help to fill, feel free to create an Issue and discuss about it before moving forward. Since this is mostly a personal guestbook, I'd appreciate a heads up before any work is started on your end!
